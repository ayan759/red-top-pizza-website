Accessibility

Last reviewed November 17, 2026

We want everyone to be able to see our menu, find us and order easily. We design and test this website against the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

What we’ve built in

  • Every page works with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline and a “Skip to main content” link.
  • Text and buttons meet WCAG colour contrast ratios, and text can be zoomed to 200% without losing content.
  • Photos have text descriptions, and decorative graphics are hidden from screen readers.
  • Form fields have visible labels, and errors explain what to fix next to the field they belong to.
  • If your device is set to reduce motion, the site’s animations and page transitions are turned off.
  • Phone numbers are tap-to-call, and opening hours are shown as text, not images.

Services we don’t control

Online ordering happens on Toast (pickup) and DoorDash (delivery), and the map on our home page comes from Google Maps. If any of these are hard to use, call us at (587) 534-5053 and we’ll take your order by phone. Our address and a Get Directions link are always shown as text next to the map.

Tell us about a barrier

If something on this site doesn’t work for you, we want to know. Email redtoppizzayyc@gmail.com or call (587) 534-5053, and tell us the page and what happened. We’ll reply and fix what we can.
