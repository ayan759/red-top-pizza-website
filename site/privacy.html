Privacy

Last updated November 17, 2026

This page explains what happens to the information you share with Red Top Pizza through redtoppizza.ca. We handle personal information in line with Alberta’s Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA).

What we collect

  • Catering quote requests. When you request a quote, we receive the details you enter: your name, email, phone number (if you give it), event date, group size and notes. We use them only to reply to you and prepare your order.
  • Emails and calls. If you contact us, we keep your message so we can answer it.

We don’t sell or rent your information, and we don’t use it for advertising.

Cookies and tracking

This website doesn’t set its own cookies and doesn’t use analytics or advertising trackers.

Other services on this site

  • Online ordering. Pickup orders are placed on Toast and delivery orders on DoorDash. Their own privacy policies apply to anything you enter there.
  • Google Maps and Google Fonts. The map on our home page and the typeface on every page load from Google, which receives your browser’s IP address when they load. Google’s privacy policy applies.
  • Social media. Links to Instagram, TikTok and Facebook take you to those sites, where their policies apply.

How long we keep it

We keep quote requests and messages only as long as we need them to serve you and keep basic business records.

Questions or corrections

To ask what information we have about you, correct it or have it deleted, email redtoppizzayyc@gmail.com or call (587) 534-5053.
