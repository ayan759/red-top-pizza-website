      What Is Detroit-Style Pizza?

      Crispy edges. Airy inside. Sauce on top.

      Born in Detroit in 1946, Detroit-style pizza is a pan-baked pizza known for its square slices, thick yet airy crust, and crunchy cheese edges. It’s a style with plenty of character—and plenty to love in every corner.

      At Red Top Pizza, here’s what makes it special:

      • The crust

        A thick, airy centre with a crisp, golden bottom.

      • The cheese

        Brick and mozzarella cheese reach the edges of the pan, where they caramelize into a deliciously crunchy border.

      • The sauce

        Our classic Detroit-style pizzas are finished with stripes of pizza sauce on top after baking.

      • The slices

        Cut into satisfying squares, with those crispy corner pieces worth reaching for first.

      About Red Top Pizza

      Great pizza brings people together. At Red Top Pizza in Sage Hill, Calgary, we’re proud to be part of those moments, from everyday family dinners to celebrations with friends.

      We specialize in premium Detroit-style pizza and offer over 14 signature pizzas, wings, calzones and more, prepared with fresh ingredients, quality toppings and premium cheese. Our Detroit-style pizza features a light, airy crust, a golden base and crispy, caramelized cheese edges, bringing bold flavour to every square.

      As a locally owned business, we care about the food we serve and the people we serve it to. Whether you’re discovering your new favourite or coming back for your usual, we look forward to making your next pizza night something special.

            Frequently asked questions, answered

            Can’t find what you need? Call us at (587) 534-5053.

            Are your meats halal?

            Yes! All meats used at Red Top Pizza are 100% halal, including our pepperoni, beef, bacon, sausage, and ham.

            What sizes do your pizzas come in?

            Our signature Detroit-style pizzas are available in two pan sizes: 4 Square, perfect for up to 2 people, and 8 Square, ideal for sharing with up to 4 people. Prefer a classic round pizza? We’ve got you covered! All our pizzas are also available in traditional 10", 12", and 14" sizes. Visit our online menu to explore all available sizes and pricing.

            Do you offer pickup and delivery?

            Yes. For pickup, order online through Toast and collect your pizza at 3950 Sage Hill Dr NW. For delivery, order through our DoorDash store. You can also call us at (587) 534-5053 to order by phone.

            Do you have vegetarian or other dietary options?

            Yes. Our vegetarian pizzas include the Veggie Supreme, Greektown and Cheese, and you can build your own with the toppings you like. The Greek and garden salads, cheesy garlic bread, jalapeño breadsticks, fries and fettuccine Alfredo are meatless too. If you have a food allergy, call us before you order and we’ll talk you through the ingredients.

            How do I place a large order?

            For up to eight pizzas, our bundles are the easiest way: order online and save. For bigger groups, school or office lunches and events, request a quote with your date and group size. You can also call us at (587) 534-5053 to place your order. The more notice you give us, the easier it is to have everything hot and ready on time.

            Can I build my own pizza?

            Yes. Our Build Your Own pizza starts with sauce and cheese, then you add the toppings you love. It comes in every size, from a 4 Square to a 14" round, and half-and-half is available on round pizzas.

            Do you offer gluten-free pizza crust?

            Yes! At Red Top Pizza, we offer gluten-free crust so more customers can enjoy their favourite pizzas. We take extra precautions when preparing gluten-free orders by using separate pans, utensils, and cutting equipment to help minimize cross-contamination. However, please note that our toppings are not certified gluten-free, and our kitchen handles gluten-containing ingredients. If you have celiac disease, a gluten allergy, or other dietary restrictions, we recommend contacting us before placing your order.

            Where are you located?

            We’re at 3950 Sage Hill Dr NW, Unit 330, in Sage Hill, Calgary. Our hours, a map and directions are in the Find Us section below.
